Welcome to my little corner of the world

Zejun Wu.

Ph.D. Candidate · Third year / University of Virginia

I am a third-year Ph.D. candidate in the Plant Ecology and Remote Sensing (PERS) Lab at the University of Virginia. I study plant ecology through remote sensing and field observations.

My current work examines forest canopy structure, including canopy depth and leaf angle distribution, using airborne LiDAR and terrestrial laser scanning (TLS). I am also interested in tropical forests.
